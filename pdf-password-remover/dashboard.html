Good to know

Only unlock what you are allowed to. Removing a password from a document you do not have the right to open may be unlawful.

AES-256, AES-128 and both RC4 variants are supported. Passwords that protect a certificate (public-key encryption) cannot be removed.

Your files are temporary. The original is deleted as soon as it is processed, and the unlocked copy is purged after 24 hours.

Storage access is scoped to your own folder, and download links are signed and short-lived.
