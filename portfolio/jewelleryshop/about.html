Our Story

Ornate Jewels was founded in 1995 by Mr. Rajesh Kanzariya with a single vision — to bring authentic, high-quality jewellery to the people of Ahmedabad at fair prices. What started as a small shop in the heart of Navrangpura has grown into one of the city's most trusted jewellery destinations.

For three generations, our family has been dedicated to the art of jewellery making. We source the finest gold, diamonds, and gemstones directly from trusted suppliers, ensuring every piece that leaves our showroom meets the highest standards of purity and craftsmanship.

Today, Ornate Jewels is proud to serve thousands of happy customers across Gujarat and beyond, offering everything from traditional gold sets to contemporary diamond creations.
