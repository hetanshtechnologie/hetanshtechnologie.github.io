📖 GST Basics

Goods and Services Tax — a comprehensive indirect tax

GST is a value-added tax levied on the supply of goods and services. Replaced multiple indirect taxes (VAT, Service Tax, Excise, etc.).

Key concepts: Supply, CGST + SGST (intra-state), IGST (inter-state), Input Tax Credit, Reverse Charge, Composition Scheme.

Threshold: ₹40L (goods), ₹20L (special category states), ₹20L (services). Registration mandatory above threshold.

Returns: GSTR-1 (outward), GSTR-3B (summary), GSTR-9 (annual).

🔄 Transition

Migration from legacy tax regime to GST

Transition Rules (Section 140-142 of CGST Act):

1. Carry forward CENVAT credit from legacy returns (last return before GST).

2. ITC on capital goods held as on 30-Jun-2017.

3. Goods returned within 6 months of sale — original tax can be adjusted.

4. Transition forms: TRAN-1 (original), TRAN-2 (additional).

5. Necessary to have filed last VAT/Service Tax/Excise returns.

🚨 Penalties & Appeals

Penalty provisions and appellate mechanism

Penalty provisions:

1. Section 122: 100% tax due for fraud/suppression (min ₹10K).

2. Section 125: ₹25K general penalty for contraventions.

3. Section 73: 10% or ₹10K (whichever higher) for non-fraud cases.

4. Late filing: ₹50/day (CGST ₹25 + SGST ₹25), max ₹5,000 per return.

Appeals: First Appeal to Joint Commissioner (3 months), Tribunal (6 months), High Court (substantial question of law), Supreme Court.

💡 Schemes

Special GST schemes for small taxpayers

1. Composition Scheme (Section 10): Turnover ≤ ₹1.5 Cr (₹75L in special states). Pay 1% (traders), 6% (restaurants), 0.5% (manufacturers). No ITC. File GSTR-4 quarterly.

2. QRMP Scheme: Quarterly Return + Monthly Payment. For turnover ≤ ₹5 Cr. File GSTR-1 quarterly (due 13th), pay tax monthly (due 25th).

3. TDS/TCS: Government depts deduct TDS @ 2%. E-commerce operators collect TCS @ 1%.

4. E-commerce Operator: Mandatory registration regardless of threshold. TCS on net value of supplies.
