Essence of the Chapter

English

Arjuna asks: who is the better devotee â€” one who worships God's personal form, or one who worships the unmanifest Absolute? Krishna answers that devotion to his personal, manifest form is the easier and more accessible path; the unmanifest is hard for embodied beings. Then he presents a graded ladder of practice: fix the mind on God, or practice constantly, or work for him with dispassion, or at the very least surrender the fruit of all action. He ends with the beautiful description of the true devotee â€” free from enmity, compassionate, equanimous, content, self-controlled, and dear to God above all.
