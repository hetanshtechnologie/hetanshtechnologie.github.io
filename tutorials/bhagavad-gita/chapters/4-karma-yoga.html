Essence of the Chapter

English

Arjuna asks: if knowledge is higher than action, why ask me to fight? Krishna answers that no one can remain without action even for a moment â€” the very nature of the world is activity. The problem is not action but attachment to its fruits. The wise act for the good of the world (loka-saá¹…graha), setting an example for others. Action offered without ego, performed as duty, becomes a means of purification. Krishna also explains desire and anger as the two great enemies that cloud judgment.
