Its eighteen chapters mirror a human journey: from the paralysis of confusion (ch. 1), through the awakening of knowledge (ch. 2), the discipline of action (ch. 3-5), the mastery of the mind (ch. 6), the vision of the divine (ch. 11), and finally to total surrender (ch. 18). Each of us meets the battlefield in our own way â€” in family, office, or inner conflict. The Gita's answer is always the same: rise, know yourself, act with love, and surrender the result. The peace it promises is not the absence of struggle but the presence of equanimity within it.
