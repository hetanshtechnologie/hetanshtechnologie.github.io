A curated e-book of the 700-verse dialogue between Lord Krishna and Arjuna — presented in Sanskrit (Devanagari + transliteration), Hindi, Gujarati and English.

संस्कृतम् — Sanskrit हिन्दी — Hindi ગુજરાતી — Gujarati English
How to read this e-book: Every chapter opens with its essence in Hindi, Gujarati and English, followed by 2–4 key shlokas (verses). Each shloka appears in Devanagari, with transliteration and translations in all four languages. Part III offers commentary and modern-day insights; Part IV has a glossary, the famous verses again, and further reading.
