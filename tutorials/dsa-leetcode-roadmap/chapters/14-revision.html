Revision Strategy

  • Re-solve each problem from scratch (no peeking at solution)
  • Compare your approach with the optimal solution
  • If you can't solve in 15 min, review the pattern and try again tomorrow
  • Track accuracy in a spreadsheet — target >80% on revision day
✏️ Exercise: On your next revision day, re-solve Two Sum, Reverse Linked List, Max Depth, Number of Islands, and Climbing Stairs without looking at solutions. Time yourself.
