Launching the Azure AI Foundry portal

The Azure portal is where resources are managed; the Foundry portal is where you actually build. It is the web application at the heart of Microsoft Foundry — the place you browse models, deploy them, chat in the playground, and build flows. This chapter shows you how to launch it and navigate to the right project, then maps the main areas of the interface.

Two Portals, One Account

It is easy to confuse the two web surfaces, so let's be explicit:

  • The Azure portal (portal.azure.com) manages infrastructure: resource groups, hubs, storage, and keys.
  • The Foundry portal (ai.azure.com) builds on that infrastructure: models, deployments, playground, and projects.

They share your sign-in identity and see the same resources — the Foundry portal just presents the AI-specific parts of them. A deployment you make in the Foundry portal appears in your Azure portal as a deployment inside the model resource, and vice versa.

Launching the Foundry Portal

There are two reliable ways to get in:

  1. Direct: go to ai.azure.com in your browser and sign in with the same Microsoft account.
  2. From Azure: open your hub's resource page in the Azure portal and click the launch button (labeled Launch Foundry or Go to Azure AI Foundry). This carries your hub context with you.

Bookmark ai.azure.com. From now on, most of your time is spent there, and it remembers which project you last used.

Signing In and Choosing a Project

After signing in, the Foundry portal shows a project picker. You will see the project you created in the previous lab — for example ai103-project — listed under its hub. If you have several, use the search box to find yours. Selecting it opens the project home, which is the main working screen for the rest of the course.

If the project picker is empty, your hub is either missing or you signed in with a different account than the one that created it. Check the account in the top-right corner first — the fix is almost always a sign-in identity issue, not a missing resource.

The Project Home

The project home screen is organized into a left navigation bar and a main content area. The left bar is where you will spend most of your time. Its sections, in the order you will use them:

  • Model catalog: the gallery of available models, covered in the next chapter.
  • Deployments: every model endpoint you have created, with status and settings.
  • Playground: interactive chat against your deployments for quick experiments.
  • Prompt flow: building multi-step application logic (used in later sections).
  • Evaluation: running tests and quality measurements against your app.
  • Connections: the data sources and services your project can reach.

How the Portal Relates to Your Resources

Keep the mapping in your head: the left menu is the front end, and each entry reads or writes the Azure resources you created in the lab.

  • Opening the model catalog and deploying a model creates a deployment in your model service resource.
  • Opening deployments lists those same deployments with their endpoints and keys.
  • Starting a playground session calls the selected deployment's endpoint with your keys.

So nothing you do in the Foundry portal is isolated — it is all real infrastructure, visible in the Azure portal, and billable if it leaves the free tiers. That is the point: the portal is a thin, friendly layer over real services.

Settings and Switching Projects

Use the project switcher in the top-left to move between projects, and the account menu in the top-right to manage sign-in. From the project settings you can see which hub and resource group the project belongs to — a good sanity check when something looks out of place.

Summary

The Foundry portal at ai.azure.com is the build surface of the course, reached directly or by launching from your hub's Azure portal page. Inside, a project groups deployments, playground, flows, evaluations, and connections, all backed by the Azure resources you created. In the next chapter you will use the model catalog to explore what models are actually available to you.
