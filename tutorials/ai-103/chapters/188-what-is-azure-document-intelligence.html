What is Azure Document Intelligence

Azure Document Intelligence is a cloud service that reads the content of documents and returns it as structured data. Given a PDF, image, or Office file, it performs OCR where needed, understands the page layout, recognizes tables and key-value pairs, and extracts the fields a business process needs — invoice totals, form answers, ID numbers, and more. Formerly known as Azure Form Recognizer, the service is the workhorse behind document automation across finance, HR, healthcare, and government workflows. This chapter explains what the service does and the models it offers.

Core Capabilities

Document Intelligence is built on four capabilities that combine into a single analysis result:

  • OCR: converts scanned text into machine-readable characters, with word-level confidence and coordinates.
  • Layout analysis: detects paragraphs, headings, tables, and figures and reports their reading order.
  • Key-value and table extraction: recognizes label-value pairs and cell grids, including tables that span pages.
  • Field extraction: pulls named fields such as invoice number, total, or issue date into a structured schema.

The service's output is a JSON document that describes the analysis: the text found, the structure that was detected, and — for prebuilt and custom models — the extracted fields with confidence scores. That JSON is what downstream code consumes, so most integrations are a single API call followed by processing the response.

Prebuilt Models

For common document types you do not need to build anything. Document Intelligence ships prebuilt models that know the fields of familiar forms:

ModelWhat it extracts
prebuilt-invoiceVendor, invoice number, issue and due dates, taxes, line items, totals
prebuilt-receiptMerchant, transaction date, line items, tax, totals
prebuilt-idDocumentNames, document numbers, dates, and country data from ID cards
prebuilt-businessCardName, title, company, phones, emails, addresses
prebuilt-readPlain text and layout with reading order

You call a prebuilt model with its model ID, such as prebuilt-invoice, and the service applies a schema trained on large volumes of that document type.

Custom Models

When a prebuilt model does not match your documents, you train a custom model:

  • Custom extraction model: you label sample documents with the fields you care about, train the model, and it learns to extract those fields from your document layouts.
  • Custom classification model: you organize samples into categories — invoice, purchase order, receipt — and the classifier assigns incoming documents to the right category, which is often the first step in a document pipeline.
  • Composed models: several extraction models combined under one ID, so a single call dispatches to whichever layout matches the document.

How It Fits the Document Pipeline

Document Intelligence is usually one stage in a larger flow: documents arrive, are classified and analyzed, and the structured output feeds downstream systems — a database, a workflow, an approval queue, or a retrieval index. It is a sibling of Azure Content Understanding: both turn documents into structured data, and choosing between them depends on the input types and the workflow you need.

AspectDocument IntelligenceContent Understanding
FocusDocument OCR, layout, and structured field extractionMultimodal analyzers for text, images, audio, and video
Prebuilt modelsRich set for forms, receipts, invoices, IDs, cardsTemplate-based analyzers and generative extraction
CustomizationTrained custom models with labeled samplesJSON-defined analyzers with typed fields
Best forClassic document automation and forms processingFlexible extraction across media types

Do not choose between these services in the abstract. If your workload is invoices, receipts, and forms, start with Document Intelligence's prebuilt models — they are tuned for exactly those documents. If your input is mixed media or the schema changes often, Content Understanding's analyzer model is often the faster fit.

Summary

Azure Document Intelligence performs OCR, layout understanding, and structured extraction, and packages them behind prebuilt and custom models. Its prebuilt models cover the most common business documents, its custom models handle everything else, and it slots cleanly into document processing pipelines.
