The Azure Free Account

An Azure free account lets you explore the platform and complete the labs in this course without paying anything. This chapter explains exactly what the free account includes, what it does not include, and the financial details you should understand before signing up. The next chapter walks through the sign-up itself.

What the Free Account Includes

The Azure free account bundles three kinds of benefits. Understanding the difference between them is important, because they expire on different schedules:

  • Credit for new services: a one-time credit (at the time of writing, US$200) that you can spend on any Azure service during the first 30 days. This is the bucket that pays for anything you create that is not otherwise free.
  • Popular free services for 12 months: a set of commonly used services — such as specific tiers of virtual machines, storage, and databases — available free for a full year, subject to monthly usage limits.
  • Always-free services: a smaller set of services available free indefinitely, with strict monthly limits designed for experimentation and low-volume use.

The credit and the 12-month benefits are measured from the day you create the account, not from when you first use them. If you sign up today and do nothing for six months, your credit and free-service window still expire on schedule.

Always-free services are the ones most relevant to this course: with modest usage, an Azure AI resource and its models can often fit inside the always-free or 12-month allowances, keeping the entire course effectively free.

Credit Card and Billing

Signing up requires a credit or debit card. Azure uses the card to verify your identity and to bill you if you exceed free allowances — it is not charged for the free tiers themselves. Three facts are worth knowing before you start:

  • You will not be charged while you stay within the free benefits.
  • You can set a spending limit (available in most free-account subscriptions) that prevents most charges once the credit is exhausted.
  • Charges only begin when you use paid services beyond the allowances, or after the credit and free windows expire.

A free account is free only while you respect its limits. Resources that do not qualify for any free tier — such as certain AI model deployments on pay-as-you-go pricing — consume your credit first and then bill your card. Follow the labs' cleanup steps and delete deployments you are not using.

What the Free Account Does Not Include

It is just as useful to know the boundaries of the free account as its benefits:

  • Not every service and every model tier has a free option.
  • The free allowances are per-account, not per-project; one account gets one set of free benefits.
  • Some capabilities — like large provisioned throughput units for high-volume AI serving — are only meaningful on paid plans.
  • After the 12-month window, the "popular free services" revert to paid pricing unless you delete them.

Upgrading Later

You are not locked in. If you later decide to run production workloads, you can upgrade the free account to a pay-as-you-go subscription at any time. The upgrade removes the spending limit and gives you access to the full catalog of services, but it does not revoke the free benefits you already have. For this course, however, you will almost certainly not need to upgrade at all.

Before You Sign Up

Gather what you need ahead of time so the sign-up flow goes smoothly:

  • A Microsoft account (an email address such as outlook.com or hotmail.com works; you can also use a personal email that becomes a Microsoft account during sign-up).
  • A phone number for identity verification.
  • A credit or debit card that can be pre-authorized for identity confirmation.
  • A supported country or region — free-account availability and free services vary by location.

Summary

The Azure free account gives you US$200 of credit for the first 30 days, a year of popular free services, and a set of always-free services. It requires a card for verification but does not charge while you stay within free allowances. With careful use — always-free tiers and disciplined cleanup — you can complete this entire course at no cost. Now let's actually create the account.
