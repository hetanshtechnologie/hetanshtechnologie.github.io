Quick look at Managing Quotas and limits

This short chapter is a practical tour of where quotas and limits actually live in the Microsoft Foundry portal. The concepts from the previous chapter — quota, TPM, RPM, and deployment rate limits — become much easier to manage once you know which screen shows what. Think of this chapter as a map of the settings, so that when your workload approaches a limit, you already know where to look.

Where to Find Quotas

The quota page lists your approved capacity for each model, in each region. To reach it:

  1. Open the Microsoft Foundry portal and go to your project.
  2. Look for Quotas under the management section of the left menu.
  3. The page shows rows for each model and region, with the quota units you can use — most commonly millions of tokens per minute for inputs and outputs.

Quota is per model and per region, not global. A quota of 500K TPM for a model in one region does nothing for a deployment of the same model in another region. When you switch regions, check the quota for that region too.

Reading the Quota Page

Each row on the quota page communicates four things:

ColumnWhat it tells you
ModelWhich model family the quota applies to
RegionWhere the quota lives
UsedHow much of the quota you are currently consuming
LimitYour approved ceiling

When the used value sits close to the limit for a model you depend on, that is your signal to plan: either reduce consumption, or request an increase before you hit the wall.

Adjusting Deployment Rate Limits

Each deployment has its own TPM and RPM values, which you can change within your quota:

  1. Open the deployment's detail page in your project.
  2. Look for the Consumption or Deployment details section.
  3. Edit the tokens-per-minute or requests-per-minute values.
  4. Save. The deployment applies the new limits immediately.

Remember the tank-and-pipe relationship: you cannot raise a deployment's rate limits beyond the quota available in that region and model. If the save fails with a quota error, raise the quota first, then retry the deployment limit.

Requesting More Quota

When your application genuinely needs more capacity, request an increase from the quota page:

  1. On the quota page, select the model and region you need more of.
  2. Click Request quota (or the equivalent action in your portal layout).
  3. Enter the new desired limit and a brief justification.
  4. Submit the request. Increases may require administrative approval, and large requests can take days, so plan ahead.

Do not request quota increases casually — they may carry commitments or be denied by your organization's policy. Exhaust cheaper options first: caching, prompt trimming, smaller models, and smarter retrieval all reduce demand before you need more capacity.

Quotas vs. Limits: A Quick Recap

  • Quota — subscription-level approved capacity, per model and region. You request increases.
  • Rate limits (TPM/RPM) — per-deployment operational caps. You adjust them within quota.
  • Service limits — platform-wide maximums you cannot exceed by request, such as the maximum number of deployments per resource.

Summary

The quota page shows your approved capacity per model and region, with used-versus-limit indicators; deployment pages let you tune TPM and RPM within that capacity; and quota increases go through a request process. Knowing these screens means a 429 or a quota error becomes a short navigation task instead of a mystery.
