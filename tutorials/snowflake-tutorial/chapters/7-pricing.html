7. Pricing Model

Compute (Credits)

Pay per second of warehouse uptime (min 60s). Credit consumption by size:

SizeCredits/hrNodes
X-Small11
Small22
Medium44
Large88

Storage & Serverless

Pay per TB/month for compressed data. Serverless features (auto-clustering, materialized views) consume credits per use.
