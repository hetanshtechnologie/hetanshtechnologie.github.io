1. Introduction to Interpersonal Skills

Getting Started

What Are Interpersonal Skills?

Interpersonal skills, also known as people skills or soft skills, are the behaviors and tactics we use to interact effectively with others. They include communication, active listening, empathy, conflict resolution, teamwork, and negotiation. These skills are essential in both personal and professional life.

Why Interpersonal Skills Matter

  • Career Success — 85% of job success comes from well-developed soft skills (Harvard, Carnegie Foundation)
  • Relationship Building — Strong interpersonal skills foster trust and collaboration
  • Conflict Reduction — Effective communication prevents and resolves misunderstandings
  • Leadership — Great leaders are distinguished by their interpersonal abilities
  • Mental Health — Positive social connections improve well-being and reduce stress

A—Z List of Interpersonal Skills

Active Listening, Adaptability, Assertiveness, Body Language, Building Trust, Collaboration, Communication, Conflict Resolution, Customer Service, Decision-Making, Diplomacy, Empathy, Emotional Intelligence, Feedback (giving & receiving), Humor, Influence, Leadership, Mediation, Mentoring, Negotiation, Networking, Persuasion, Positive Attitude, Rapport Building, Respect, Social Awareness, Teamwork, Verbal Communication, Work Ethic, Written Communication.

Self-Assessment

Before diving in, rate yourself on a scale of 1-5 for each skill: listening, empathy, conflict resolution, teamwork, verbal communication, non-verbal communication, negotiation, and decision-making. Identify 2-3 areas to focus on throughout this tutorial.

Practice Task: Complete the self-assessment above. Ask a trusted friend or colleague to rate you on the same skills. Compare the results — where do you agree and disagree? Choose one skill to work on this week and set a specific goal (e.g., "I will practice active listening in at least two conversations today").
