13. Practice & Resources

Practice & Resources

Self-Assessment Tools

  • Communication Style Quiz — Identify if you're assertive, passive, aggressive, or passive-aggressive
  • EQ Self-Assessment — Goleman's Emotional Intelligence self-assessment (available online)
  • Listening Skills Inventory — Rate your listening habits on a scale of 1-5
  • Conflict Style Assessment — Thomas-Kilmann Instrument (TKI) identifies your default style

30-Day Interpersonal Skills Challenge

  • Week 1 (Listening): Practice active listening daily. Paraphrase at least once per conversation
  • Week 2 (Empathy): Before responding in any conversation, ask yourself "what might they be feeling?"
  • Week 3 (Conflict): Use the 6-step resolution process on any disagreement
  • Week 4 (Integration): Combine all skills — lead a meeting, give feedback, negotiate a win-win

Interview Preparation

Practice answers to common behavioral questions using STAR: "Tell me about a time you resolved a conflict," "Describe a situation where you worked in a team," "Give an example of when you showed leadership." Prepare specific examples from your experience.

Study Plan (6 Weeks)

  • Communication fundamentals, active listening, non-verbal cues
  • Emotional intelligence, self-awareness, empathy
  • Conflict resolution, mediation, difficult conversations
  • Teamwork, collaboration, meeting skills
  • Decision-making, problem-solving, critical thinking
  • Negotiation, persuasion, integration and practice

Bootcamp & Certification

  • LinkedIn Learning — Communication Foundations, Conflict Resolution courses
  • Coursera — "Improving Communication Skills" (University of Pennsylvania)
  • edX — "Emotional Intelligence at Work" (Berkeley)
  • Toastmasters — Public speaking and leadership development
  • Dale Carnegie — Effective Communications and Human Relations
Final Challenge: Complete the 30-day challenge. At the end, ask three people who know you well whether they've noticed any changes in your interpersonal skills. Write a self-reflection on your growth, areas for continued improvement, and your plan for ongoing development.
