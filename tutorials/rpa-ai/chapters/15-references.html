Design Patterns Cheatsheet

  • Read → Structure → Enter: extract from documents/emails, then enter into systems.
  • Classify → Route: understand a message/document and route it.
  • Score → Decide → Escalate: predict risk/quality, then act or escalate by confidence.
  • Listen → Draft → Respond: understand incoming communication and generate a response.
  • Monitor → Alert → Act: watch signals, detect anomalies, trigger actions.
  • Human-in-the-loop: confidence gates, exception queues, approvals, correction loops.

Final Challenge

Build one complete intelligent automation, end to end: an email (or file) trigger → AI understanding (document AI or LLM) → validation with a confidence gate → RPA action into a spreadsheet/system → exception queue for uncertain items → a summary email with metrics. If it runs for a week unattended, logs every decision, and you can explain each of its governance controls, you are ready to build production automation.
