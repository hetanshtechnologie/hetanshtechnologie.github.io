A comprehensive guide to SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) and SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response) — the backbone of modern Security Operations Centers. From log collection and correlation rules to playbook automation and incident response, this book explains concepts, architecture, real-world workflows, and hands-on exercises for building a career in security operations.
