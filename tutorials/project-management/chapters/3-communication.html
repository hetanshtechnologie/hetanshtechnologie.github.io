3. Communication in Project Management

Communication

Why Communication Matters

Project managers spend up to 90% of their time communicating. Poor communication is the #1 cause of project failure. Effective communication ensures stakeholders are informed, aligned, and engaged throughout the project lifecycle.

Communication Channels

The number of communication channels grows exponentially with team size: Channels = n(n-1)/2 where n is the number of stakeholders. A team of 5 has 10 channels; a team of 10 has 45 channels.

Communication Methods

  • Push Communication — Sender pushes information to recipients (emails, reports, newsletters)
  • Pull Communication — Recipients access information on demand (SharePoint, wikis, dashboards)
  • Interactive Communication — Real-time, two-way exchange (meetings, calls, video conferences)

The Communication Model

Sender → Encoding → Message → Medium → Decoding → Receiver → Feedback. Noise can disrupt any stage of this model. Effective communicators minimize noise through clarity, active listening, and confirmation loops.

Communication Management Plan

A key artifact in project planning that defines:

  • What information needs to be communicated
  • When and how often to communicate
  • Who receives the information
  • Who sends the information
  • Which communication methods and channels to use
  • Escalation procedures for issues

Effective Communication Skills

  • Active Listening — Focus fully, understand, respond thoughtfully
  • Clear and Concise — Use simple language, avoid jargon
  • Empathy — Understand others' perspectives and emotions
  • Non-Verbal — Body language, tone, eye contact matter
  • Feedback — Provide constructive, timely, specific feedback

Presentations

Project managers frequently present status updates, proposals, and reports. Best practices include knowing your audience, structuring with a clear narrative, using visuals effectively, and rehearsing key points.

Practice Task: Create a Communication Management Plan for a project with 8 team members and 3 external stakeholders. Identify all communication channels, choose appropriate methods for different message types, and define a weekly reporting cadence. Practice active listening in your next team conversation.
