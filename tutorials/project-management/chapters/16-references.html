Best Practices Cheat Sheet

  • Always start with a project charter — never work without authorization
  • Decompose scope into a WBS before estimating
  • Identify risks early and revisit them regularly
  • Communicate proactively — over-communicate rather than under-communicate
  • Track actuals against baselines and take corrective action early
  • Celebrate milestones and recognize team contributions
  • Document lessons learned throughout the project
  • Always close projects formally, not by abandonment

Q&A

Q: Do I need a certification to be a project manager?
A: Not necessarily. Many PMs enter the field through experience. However, certifications validate your knowledge and can accelerate career growth.

Q: Which methodology is best?
A: There is no single best methodology. Choose the approach that fits your project's size, complexity, uncertainty, and organizational culture.

Q: What software should I learn?
A: Microsoft Project and Jira are industry standards. Learn Excel for EVM analysis, and at least one collaboration tool (Slack, Teams, Asana, Trello).

Q: How do I handle difficult stakeholders?
A: Listen actively, understand their interests, communicate frequently, manage expectations, and use a stakeholder engagement plan to address concerns systematically.
