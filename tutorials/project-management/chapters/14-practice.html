14. Practice & Certification

Practice & Certification

Capstone Project

Apply everything you've learned by planning a complete project from start to finish. Choose one of the following:

  • Option A: Organizing a 3-day tech conference with 500 attendees
  • Option B: Building a mobile app for a local business
  • Option C: Implementing a new CRM system across a company with 200 employees

Capstone Deliverables

  1. Project Charter with business case
  2. Scope Statement and WBS (minimum 20 work packages)
  3. Schedule with Gantt chart and critical path identified
  4. Cost estimate and budget baseline with 10% contingency
  5. Risk Register with 10 risks, analysis, and response plans
  6. Communication Management Plan
  7. RACI Chart for key deliverables
  8. Quality Management Plan
  9. Project closure report with lessons learned

Quiz Questions

  1. What is the difference between risk mitigation and risk avoidance?
  2. Calculate the critical path for a project with 6 tasks and given dependencies.
  3. How does Earned Value Management integrate scope, schedule, and cost?
  4. Compare Agile and Waterfall methodologies. When would you use each?
  5. What is the purpose of a RACI chart and how do you assign accountability?
  6. Explain the difference between Quality Control and Quality Assurance.
  7. How do you handle scope creep when a stakeholder requests a new feature?
  8. What is the Rule of Seven in control charts?

Study Plan (8 Weeks)

  • Introduction, project lifecycle, organizational structures
  • Scope and requirements management, WBS
  • Scheduling (CPM, PERT, Gantt charts)
  • Cost management and EVM
  • Risk management and quality management
  • Communication, HR, and stakeholder management
  • Procurement, contracts, and advanced topics
  • Capstone project, review, and exam prep

Certification Path

  • PMP (Project Management Professional) — PMI's gold standard; requires 35 PDUs and 4,500 hours of experience
  • CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management) — Entry-level certification from PMI
  • PRINCE2 Foundation/Practitioner — Popular in Europe and government sectors
  • Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP) — Agile-specific certification
  • CSM (Certified ScrumMaster) — Scrum Alliance certification
  • Six Sigma Green/Black Belt — Quality-focused certification
Tip: Start with the CAPM if you're new to PM. The PMP requires significant documented experience but is highly valued by employers worldwide.
Final Challenge: Complete the capstone project of your choice with all 9 deliverables. Review the quiz questions and ensure you can answer each one confidently. Identify which certification aligns with your career goals and create a study plan to achieve it within 6 months.
