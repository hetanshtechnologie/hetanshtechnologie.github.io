Break It Down

Introduction — Big goals often feel intimidating. Writing a book, launching a business, or mastering a new skill can seem overwhelming when viewed as one giant mountain. The secret is simple: break it down into smaller steps.

Story / Analogy — Imagine trying to eat a whole cake in one bite. Impossible, right? But slice it into pieces, and suddenly it’s easy to enjoy. Goals work the same way — slice them into steps, and you’ll finish what once felt impossible.

Practical Steps to Break Down Goals
  1. Define the Big Goal — Write it clearly.
  2. Identify Major Milestones — Outline, draft, edit, publish.
  3. Divide Each Milestone into Tasks — Small, actionable steps.
  4. Set Deadlines for Each Step — Assign realistic dates.
  5. Celebrate Small Wins — Acknowledge progress after each step.
Reflection Exercise
Write down one big goal. Break it into 5 milestones. For each milestone, list 3 small tasks you can do this week.

Deadlines are not restrictions — they are accelerators. They push you to act, sharpen your focus, and build discipline. When you respect deadlines, you respect your future.
