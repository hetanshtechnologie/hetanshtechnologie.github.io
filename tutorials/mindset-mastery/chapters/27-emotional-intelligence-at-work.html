Emotional Intelligence at Work

Introduction — Technical skills may get you hired, but emotional intelligence (EQ) determines how far you go. EQ is the ability to understand and manage your own emotions while recognizing and influencing the emotions of others.

Practical Steps
  1. Practice Self Awareness
  2. Develop Empathy
  3. Manage Reactions — Pause before responding in tense situations.
  4. Improve Communication — Use “I” statements.
  5. Build Social Skills
Reflection Exercise
Recall a recent workplace conflict. Write down how you reacted emotionally. Identify one way to respond with greater EQ next time.

Emotional intelligence isn’t soft — it’s strategic. It turns tension into trust, mistakes into learning, and teams into communities.
