The Discipline of Reflection

Introduction — Reflection is the practice of pausing to learn from experience. Without reflection, lessons are lost, mistakes repeat, and growth stalls.

Practical Steps
  1. Schedule Time
  2. Ask Key Questions
  3. Write It Down
  4. Seek Feedback
  5. Apply Lessons
Reflection Exercise
Write down one recent challenge. Reflect: What did I learn? Identify one change you’ll make next time.

Reflection turns experience into wisdom. It’s the discipline of learning from the past to shape the future.
