The Joy of Contribution

Introduction — Success feels incomplete if it’s only about personal gain. True fulfillment comes from contribution — giving your time, skills, or resources to help others.

Story / Analogy — Think of a candle. It loses nothing by lighting another candle, yet it spreads more light.

Practical Steps
  1. Share Knowledge
  2. Give Time
  3. Offer Encouragement
  4. Support Growth
  5. Practice Daily Generosity
Reflection Exercise
Write down one way you can contribute to someone’s life this week. Commit to acting on it.

Contribution transforms success into significance. When you give, you don’t lose — you multiply. The joy of contribution is the joy of knowing your life made a difference.
