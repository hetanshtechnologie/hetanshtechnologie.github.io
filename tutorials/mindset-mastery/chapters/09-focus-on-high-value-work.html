Focus on High Value Work

Introduction — Not all work is created equal. Some tasks deliver massive results, while others barely move the needle. The secret to extraordinary productivity is simple: spend most of your time on high value work.

Why High Value Work Matters — The 80/20 Rule: 20% of tasks produce 80% of results. High value tasks often influence multiple areas of your life at once. Opportunity cost: every minute spent on low value work is a minute stolen from high value work.

Story / Analogy — Imagine two employees. One spends hours perfecting PowerPoint fonts. The other spends hours meeting clients and closing deals. Who creates more value? The difference isn’t effort — it’s focus on what matters most.

Practical Steps
  1. List All Tasks — Write down everything you do in a week.
  2. Rank by Impact — Which tasks move me closer to my biggest goals?
  3. Eliminate or Delegate Low Value Work
  4. Schedule High Value Work First
  5. Review Weekly
Reflection Exercise
Write down 5 tasks you did today. Circle the one that created the most impact. Commit to doing that type of task first tomorrow.

Success doesn’t come from doing more — it comes from doing what matters most. When you focus on high value work, you multiply your results, accelerate your growth, and create a life of meaningful achievement.
