Integrity as Your Foundation

Introduction — Success built without integrity eventually collapses. Integrity is the foundation of trust, credibility, and lasting achievement.

Story / Analogy — Imagine a leader who cuts corners for quick gains. At first, results look good, but eventually trust erodes, and the team falls apart. Contrast that with a leader who consistently acts with honesty and fairness. Their success may take longer, but it lasts.

Practical Steps
  1. Define Your Values
  2. Keep Your Word
  3. Act Transparently
  4. Align Actions with Beliefs
  5. Hold Yourself Accountable
Reflection Exercise
Write down one recent decision. Ask: Did it align with my values? If not, identify how to act with greater integrity next time.
