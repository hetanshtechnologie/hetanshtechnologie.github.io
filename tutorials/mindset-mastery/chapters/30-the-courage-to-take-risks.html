The Courage to Take Risks

Introduction — Playing it safe may feel comfortable, but it rarely leads to greatness. Every breakthrough, innovation, and success story is built on risk.

Story / Analogy — Consider an entrepreneur launching a new product. The risk of failure is real, but so is the potential for success. Without courage, the idea stays a dream. With courage, it becomes a reality that could change lives.

Practical Steps
  1. Start Small
  2. Assess, Don’t Avoid
  3. Reframe Failure
  4. Build Confidence Through Preparation
  5. Celebrate Boldness
Reflection Exercise
Write down one risk you’ve been avoiding. List the potential benefits. Commit to taking one step toward it this week.

Courage isn’t about reckless leaps — it’s about intentional risks that move you forward. Risk is the price of progress.
