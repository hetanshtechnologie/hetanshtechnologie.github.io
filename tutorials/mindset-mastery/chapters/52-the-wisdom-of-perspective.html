The Wisdom of Perspective

Introduction — Perspective is the lens through which we see life. Two people can face the same challenge, yet one sees defeat while the other sees opportunity.

Practical Steps
  1. Pause Before Reacting
  2. Seek Multiple Viewpoints
  3. Reframe Challenges
  4. Practice Gratitude
  5. Visualize the Bigger Picture
Reflection Exercise
Write down one current challenge. List three different perspectives you could take. Choose the one that empowers you most.

Perspective is wisdom in action. It doesn’t change reality, but it changes how you experience it.
