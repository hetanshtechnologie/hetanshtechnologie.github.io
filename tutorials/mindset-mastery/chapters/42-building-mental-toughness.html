Building Mental Toughness

Introduction — Mental toughness is the ability to stay strong, focused, and resilient under pressure. It’s not about avoiding challenges but about facing them with grit and determination.

Story / Analogy — Picture a marathon runner hitting the “wall” at mile 20. Their body screams to stop, but their mind pushes them forward. That’s mental toughness.

Practical Steps
  1. Embrace Challenges
  2. Control Self Talk
  3. Practice Discipline
  4. Visualize Success
  5. Stay Calm Under Pressure
Reflection Exercise
Write down one challenge you’re facing. Identify how mental toughness could change your response.

Mental toughness isn’t about being unbreakable — it’s about being unshakable. It’s the grit that keeps you moving.
