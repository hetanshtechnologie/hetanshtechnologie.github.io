The Courage to Be Authentic

Introduction — Authenticity is the courage to live as your true self, not the version others expect. It means aligning your actions with your values, speaking your truth, and refusing to wear masks for approval.

Practical Steps
  1. Know Your Values
  2. Speak Your Truth
  3. Drop the Masks
  4. Align Actions with Beliefs
  5. Accept Imperfections
Reflection Exercise
Write down one area where you feel you’re not being authentic. Identify one step to align it with your true self.

Authenticity takes courage, but it rewards you with freedom. When you live authentically, you stop chasing approval and start living with purpose.
