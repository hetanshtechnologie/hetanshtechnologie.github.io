The Value of Humility

Introduction — Humility isn’t weakness — it’s wisdom. It’s the quiet strength of knowing your worth without needing to prove it.

Story / Analogy — Think of a tree. The taller it grows, the deeper its roots must be. Humility is those roots.

Practical Steps
  1. Listen More Than You Speak
  2. Acknowledge Mistakes
  3. Celebrate Others
  4. Stay Curious
  5. Serve Without Recognition
Reflection Exercise
Write down one recent success. Reflect on who helped you achieve it. Express gratitude to them this week.

Humility doesn’t diminish you — it elevates you. It’s the strength to stay grounded, the wisdom to keep learning, and the grace to lift others.
