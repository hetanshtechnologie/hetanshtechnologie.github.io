The Habit of Lifelong Learning

Introduction — Learning doesn’t end with school — it’s a lifelong journey. The world changes constantly, and those who keep learning stay ahead.

Practical Steps
  1. Read Daily — Even 15 minutes a day adds up.
  2. Take Courses
  3. Learn from People
  4. Practice Curiosity
  5. Apply What You Learn
Reflection Exercise
Write down one skill you’ve always wanted to learn. Identify one resource to start with. Commit to 30 minutes this week learning it.

Lifelong learning isn’t a hobby — it’s a mindset. When you stay curious and keep growing, you remain adaptable, confident, and prepared.
