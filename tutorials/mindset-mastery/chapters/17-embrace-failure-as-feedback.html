Embrace Failure as Feedback

Introduction — Failure isn’t the opposite of success — it’s part of success. Every setback carries a lesson, every mistake reveals a path forward. The difference between those who grow and those who quit lies in perspective: winners see failure as feedback, not defeat.

Story / Analogy — Thomas Edison famously said, “I have not failed. I’ve just found 10,000 ways that won’t work.” His perspective turned failure into progress. Without those “failures,” the light bulb would never have been invented.

Practical Steps
  1. Redefine Failure — See it as feedback, not finality.
  2. Detach Ego from Outcome
  3. Document Lessons
  4. Share the Story — Talk about your failures openly.
  5. Try Again, Smarter
Reflection Exercise
Recall a recent failure. Write down three lessons it taught you. Decide one action you’ll take differently next time.

Failure is not the end — it’s the beginning of wisdom. When you embrace failure as feedback, you transform setbacks into stepping stones.
