Self Reflection and Journaling

Introduction — Growth doesn’t just come from action — it comes from reflection. Self reflection allows you to pause, evaluate, and learn from your experiences. Journaling is the most powerful tool for reflection.

Practical Steps
  1. Set Aside Time — 10–15 minutes daily or weekly.
  2. Ask Powerful Questions — What went well? What could I improve? What did I learn?
  3. Write Freely
  4. Track Patterns
  5. Review Regularly
Reflection Exercise
Write a journal entry tonight answering: What did I accomplish today? What challenged me? What lesson can I carry forward?

Self reflection and journaling are your personal compass. They don’t just record your journey — they guide it.
