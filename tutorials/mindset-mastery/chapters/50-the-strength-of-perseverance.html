The Strength of Perseverance

Introduction — Perseverance is the ability to keep moving forward despite obstacles, failures, or delays. It’s not about speed — it’s about endurance.

Story / Analogy — Consider Thomas Edison, who tested thousands of materials before inventing the light bulb. Each failure was a lesson, not a defeat. His perseverance turned persistence into innovation.

Practical Steps
  1. Set Long Term Goals
  2. Break Goals into Small Steps
  3. Embrace Failure as Feedback
  4. Stay Disciplined
  5. Celebrate Endurance
Reflection Exercise
Write down one goal you’ve considered quitting. Reflect on why it matters. Commit to one action this week.

Perseverance is strength in motion. It’s the refusal to quit, the courage to continue, and the determination to finish.
