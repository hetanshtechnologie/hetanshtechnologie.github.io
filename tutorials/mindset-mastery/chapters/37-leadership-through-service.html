Leadership Through Service

Introduction — Leadership isn’t about titles or authority — it’s about service. The greatest leaders lead by lifting others, not by standing above them.

Practical Steps
  1. Listen Actively
  2. Empower Others
  3. Celebrate Contributions
  4. Provide Support
  5. Lead by Example
Reflection Exercise
Write down one way you can serve your team or community this week. Act on it.

Leadership isn’t about being in charge — it’s about taking care of those in your charge. Service transforms leadership from authority into influence.
