Delegate and Elevate

Introduction — You don’t have to do everything yourself. In fact, trying to do it all is the fastest way to burn out. True productivity comes from focusing on the tasks only you can do — and delegating the rest.

Story / Analogy — Imagine a CEO who insists on answering every customer email personally. Admirable, but unsustainable. By delegating to a support team, the CEO can focus on vision, growth, and leadership — the tasks only they can do.

Practical Steps to Delegate Effectively
  1. Identify Tasks to Delegate — Routine, repetitive, or low value tasks.
  2. Choose the Right Person — Match tasks to strengths.
  3. Provide Clear Instructions — Define outcome, deadline, expectations.
  4. Trust and Verify — Allow freedom, check progress at milestones.
  5. Elevate Your Focus — Use freed time for strategic, high impact work.

Delegation isn’t weakness — it’s wisdom. By trusting others with tasks, you elevate yourself to focus on what truly matters. When you delegate, you don’t just lighten your load; you multiply your impact.
