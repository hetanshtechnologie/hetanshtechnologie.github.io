The Long Term Vision

Introduction — Short term wins feel good, but they don’t build lasting success. True achievement comes from having a long term vision — a clear picture of where you want to go and why.

Story / Analogy — Imagine two sailors. One sails aimlessly, chasing the wind. The other charts a course toward a destination. The second sailor may face storms, but they always know where they’re headed. Vision is your destination.

Practical Steps
  1. Define Your Destination
  2. Write It Down
  3. Break It Into Milestones
  4. Align Daily Actions
  5. Review Regularly
Reflection Exercise
Write down your vision for the next 10 years. Identify one milestone you can achieve in the next 12 months. Commit to one daily action that aligns with that milestone.

Vision is the anchor that keeps you steady and the compass that keeps you moving. When you live with long term vision, every step gains meaning.
