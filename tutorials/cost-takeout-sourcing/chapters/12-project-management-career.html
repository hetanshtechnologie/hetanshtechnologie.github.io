12. Workstream Management, Practice & Career

Delivering and Growing

Managing Multiple Workstreams

Consulting engagements rarely have a single thread. You may run several categories, multiple clients, and practice-building activities at once. Key practices:

  • Workplan with owners — every initiative has an owner, deliverable, and date.
  • Weekly operating rhythm — status, risks, decisions needed, next actions.
  • Risks and issues log — surfaced early, with owners and due dates.
  • Capacity management — be realistic about what can be delivered; quality beats volume.
  • Escalation discipline — know when a decision must go to the steering committee.

Delivering Under Tight Timelines

  • Scope ruthlessly: define what "done" means per deliverable.
  • Use parallel workstreams where dependencies allow.
  • Prototype outputs early and iterate with client feedback.
  • Protect the critical path — move non-critical work off it.
  • Communicate progress honestly; surprise is the enemy of trust.

Business Development

Advisory careers include business development: responding to proposals, preparing client presentations, and growing accounts. The essentials:

  • Proposals — client challenge, approach, team, timeline, value, and commercial terms
  • Client presentations — credibility first: show you understand their business and data
  • Account growth — deliver, then expand: adjacent categories, transformation, ongoing managed services
  • Networking — relationships across client and industry stakeholders

Thought Leadership & Practice Development

Contributing to the practice grows the firm and your reputation: publish point-of-view articles, build reusable tools (sourcing playbooks, should-cost models, dashboard templates), contribute to proposals, and speak at industry events. Strong practitioners leave the practice stronger than they found it.

Mentoring Junior Consultants

Mentoring is part of the role: assign meaningful work, give structured feedback, model client behavior, and coach on structuring, storytelling, and stakeholder handling. A team's capability is the multiplier on its leader's own delivery.
