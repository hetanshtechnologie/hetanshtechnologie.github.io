Sample Job Description

Role: S&P - Cost TakeOut | Ahmedabad | Supply Chain & Network Operations

Team: Enterprise Technology & Performance (ET&P). Enterprise technology must do much more than keep the wheels turning; it is the engine that drives functional excellence and the enabler of innovation and long-term growth.

Key responsibilities: Lead client engagements in strategic sourcing, procurement transformation, and cost optimization; advise on sourcing strategy, supplier selection, contracting, and operating models; conduct spend analysis, category assessments, and market intelligence; run end-to-end sourcing initiatives (RFx, negotiations, supplier onboarding, contract finalization); support process redesign, policy, and governance; manage stakeholders across procurement, finance, legal, operations, and business; build business cases and executive presentations; contribute to practice development and mentoring; support business development.

Key skills: Strategic sourcing and category management; procurement transformation and cost reduction; contract and supplier negotiation; spend analytics and financial modeling; stakeholder management and executive communication; problem structuring; procurement tools (e-sourcing, ERP, spend analytics); project management under tight timelines; commercial acumen and supply risk awareness; consulting mindset.

Interview Questions

  1. Walk me through a sourcing engagement and the savings you delivered. How was the saving verified?
  2. How do you prioritize categories in a spend analysis?
  3. How would you negotiate a 10% price reduction from a reluctant incumbent supplier?
  4. What is the difference between savings and value creation?
  5. How do you handle a stakeholder who blocks a sourcing recommendation?
  6. What tools have you used across source-to-pay, and which did you find most effective?
  7. How do you build a should-cost model, and what data do you need?
  8. How do you ensure savings are sustained after the project ends?
  9. Describe a time you managed multiple workstreams under tight deadlines.
  10. How do you structure an executive presentation for a CFO?
