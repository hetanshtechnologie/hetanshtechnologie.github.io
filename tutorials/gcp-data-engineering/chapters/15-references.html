Final Challenge

Build one complete pipeline, end to end, in your lab: load public data into GCS → land it in BigQuery → transform with dbt (with tests) → schedule it with Airflow → reconcile the output against a direct SQL baseline. If it runs unattended for a week without manual intervention and you can show its lineage and cost, you are ready for a production data team.
