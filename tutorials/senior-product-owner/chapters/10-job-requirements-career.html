10. Job Requirements & Career Path

Career & Requirements

Educational Background

A Bachelor's degree in Business, Technology, Engineering, Finance, or a related field is typically required. Many professionals also hold advanced degrees (MBA, M.Tech) or specialized finance/tech qualifications that accelerate progression.

Experience Requirements

  • Significant experience in Product Ownership, Product Management, Payments, Financial Services, or Technology Delivery
  • Proven track record of managing complex products and delivering large-scale change initiatives
  • Experience working with cross-functional stakeholders across business, operations, and technology domains
  • Experience in payments, transaction banking, cash management, or financial services products
  • Experience delivering regulatory or compliance-driven change initiatives

Core Competencies

CompetencyWhat It Involves
Product Lifecycle ManagementOwning products from ideation through optimization
Agile DeliveryWorking in Scrum/SAFe environments, backlog ownership
Analytical ThinkingData-driven decision-making and problem solving
CommunicationClear written and verbal communication at all levels
NegotiationBalancing priorities and reaching agreements
Stakeholder ManagementBuilding trust across business, operations, and technology

Technical & Domain Knowledge

  • Knowledge of payment processing and transaction lifecycle
  • Understanding of SWIFT messaging (MT and MX formats)
  • Knowledge of ISO 20022 financial messaging standard
  • Familiarity with cash management and transaction banking products
  • Understanding of regulatory frameworks (AML, sanctions, data protection)

Career Progression

  1. Business Analyst / Junior PO — Requirements, analysis, story writing
  2. Product Owner — Own a product area or feature set
  3. Senior Product Owner — Own complex platforms, cross-functional leadership
  4. Product Manager / Lead PO — Portfolio-level strategy and market focus
  5. Principal / Head of Product — Product organization leadership

Interview Preparation

  • Be ready to explain product lifecycle with concrete examples
  • Describe how you prioritize a backlog with competing demands
  • Walk through a regulatory change you delivered
  • Show how you measure product success
  • Demonstrate stakeholder management with STAR stories
Career Tip: Combine product ownership skills with payments domain knowledge. Understanding ISO 20022, SWIFT, and real-time payment rails makes you far more valuable to banks and fintechs than a generic product owner.
Practice Task: Map your current skills against the job requirements in this chapter. Identify 3 gaps and create a 90-day development plan covering certifications, domain knowledge, and hands-on practice.
