9. Post-Implementation Support

Post-Launch Support

Acting as an Escalation Point

The Senior Product Owner acts as an escalation point for significant issues during post-implementation support periods. Escalation is triggered when issues exceed normal support thresholds — for example, major incidents, regulatory concerns, or customer-impacting defects.

Effective Escalation Handling

  1. Acknowledge the issue and its severity quickly
  2. Bring the right teams together (technology, operations, risk)
  3. Protect the customer and manage communications
  4. Drive root cause analysis and fix planning
  5. Communicate status transparently to stakeholders
  6. Document lessons learned

Monitoring Production Performance

After launch, the Product Owner monitors production performance and validates expected business and operational outcomes. Monitoring covers:

  • Transaction volumes and throughput
  • Error and rejection rates
  • STP and automation levels
  • System availability and response times
  • Customer feedback and complaint trends
  • Adoption and usage of new features
Post-Launch Support Window: A defined period (e.g., 2-4 weeks) of enhanced monitoring and rapid response after release. During this window, issues are prioritized and resolved faster than business-as-usual.

Leading Continuous Improvement

The Senior Product Owner leads continuous improvement initiatives based on lessons learned and stakeholder feedback. Improvement opportunities often come from:

  • Post-implementation reviews
  • Incident root-cause analysis
  • Customer and operations feedback
  • Performance data trends
  • Audit findings

Partnering to Resolve Issues and Optimize

The Product Owner partners with operational and technology teams to resolve issues and optimize product performance. This collaboration:

  • Ensures fixes are prioritized and delivered
  • Keeps the backlog updated with optimization items
  • Bridges operational realities with technical solutions
  • Prevents recurrence through systemic fixes
Example: Two weeks after launch, a reconciliation break is detected. The Product Owner escalates, convenes technology and operations, confirms the cause is a timing difference in statement processing, and coordinates a fix plus a control to prevent recurrence. A post-launch review documents the lessons for future releases.
Practice Task: Draft a post-implementation review template with sections for objectives, outcomes, issues, lessons learned, and follow-up actions. Complete it for a recent product release you know about.
