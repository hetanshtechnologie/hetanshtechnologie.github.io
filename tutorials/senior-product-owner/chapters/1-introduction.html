1. Introduction to the Senior Product Owner Role

Getting Started

The Role in Brief

The Senior Product Owner is responsible for defining, prioritizing, and delivering product enhancements and strategic initiatives across payment processing platforms and related operational capabilities. The role bridges business, operations, technology, risk, and compliance functions to ensure products and services are customer-focused, operationally efficient, compliant with regulatory requirements, and aligned with organizational strategy.

A successful Senior Product Owner drives product vision, roadmap execution, operational readiness, stakeholder engagement, and end-to-end product lifecycle management while ensuring solutions deliver measurable business and customer value.

Core Idea: A Senior Product Owner is the bridge between strategy and delivery. They decide what to build and why, while delivery teams figure out how to build it.

The Team Context

Customer is about much more than just the numbers. It's about attesting to accomplishments and challenges and helping to assure strong foundations for future aspirations. The organization exemplifies what, how, and why of change so you're always ready to act ahead. Learn more about Customer — the team operates at the intersection of advisory, technology, and customer-facing solutions.

Why Product Ownership Matters in Payments

  • Complexity — Payment platforms involve many stakeholders: customers, banks, regulators, technology teams, and operations.
  • Regulatory Pressure — Payment products must comply with evolving regulations and industry standards (e.g., RBI, SWIFT, ISO 20022).
  • Customer Expectations — Users demand fast, secure, and frictionless payment experiences.
  • Business Value — Every product decision has direct impact on revenue, cost, and customer satisfaction.
  • Operational Efficiency — Straight-through processing (STP) and automation reduce cost-to-serve and error rates.

Real-World Example: A large bank rolls out ISO 20022 messaging across its payment platform. The Senior Product Owner coordinates with technology (new message formats), operations (exception handling), risk (sanctions screening), and compliance (regulatory reporting) to deliver the change on time without disrupting daily payment processing.
