Sample Job Description

The Senior Product Owner is responsible for defining, prioritizing, and delivering product enhancements and strategic initiatives across payment processing platforms and related operational capabilities. The role bridges business, operations, technology, risk, and compliance functions to ensure products and services are customer-focused, operationally efficient, compliant with regulatory requirements, and aligned with organizational strategy.

Interview Questions

  1. Describe a product you managed end-to-end. What was your role at each stage?
  2. How do you decide what goes on top of your backlog?
  3. Tell me about a time you delivered a regulatory or compliance-driven change.
  4. How do you handle a stakeholder who demands a feature you believe has low value?
  5. How do you measure whether a product enhancement delivered business value?
  6. What is your experience with ISO 20022 or SWIFT messaging?
  7. How do you ensure operational readiness before launch?
  8. Describe how you use KPIs to drive product improvement.

Study Plan (8 Weeks)

  • Weeks 1-2 — Product ownership fundamentals; read this guide's strategy, lifecycle, and stakeholder chapters
  • Weeks 3-4 — Payments domain: payment rails, transaction banking, cash management
  • Weeks 5-6 — SWIFT and ISO 20022: read standards, explore message examples
  • Week 7 — Agile practice: run a small Scrum project; explore SAFe fundamentals
  • Week 8 — Regulatory awareness: AML, sanctions, open banking; prepare for certifications
