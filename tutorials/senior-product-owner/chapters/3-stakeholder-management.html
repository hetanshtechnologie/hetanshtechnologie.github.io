3. Stakeholder Management

Stakeholder Management

Partnering Across the Organization

The Senior Product Owner partners with business, operations, technology, risk, compliance, and customer-facing teams to align product priorities and delivery objectives. Effective stakeholder management is critical because payment products touch nearly every part of the organization.

Building Strong Relationships

Strong stakeholder relationships are built on trust, transparency, and reliability. Practices that help:

  • Listen actively — Understand each stakeholder's goals, pressures, and constraints.
  • Communicate consistently — Regular status updates, both formal and informal.
  • Be reliable — Follow through on commitments and acknowledge mistakes.
  • Show empathy — Acknowledge the impact of change on different teams.
  • Bring data — Support proposals with evidence and business cases.

Facilitating Cross-Functional Decision-Making

The Senior Product Owner facilitates discussions and decision-making across cross-functional teams. This involves:

  1. Defining the decision to be made and its scope
  2. Gathering relevant facts, options, and trade-offs
  3. Inviting diverse perspectives
  4. Driving toward a clear, documented decision
  5. Communicating the outcome and next steps
Decision-Making Framework: Use a RACI chart to clarify who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each key decision. Escalate when consensus cannot be reached.

Challenging Existing Processes and Practices

Senior Product Owners are expected to challenge existing processes and practices where improvements can enhance customer outcomes, operational efficiency, or scalability. Constructive challenge requires:

  • Understanding why the current process exists
  • Identifying the pain points and cost of the status quo
  • Proposing alternatives with evidence
  • Respecting governance while pushing for change
  • Building allies before making the case

Balancing Competing Stakeholder Priorities

Stakeholders often have conflicting priorities. The Senior Product Owner balances these while maintaining alignment to strategic objectives. Techniques include:

  • Objective scoring — Score proposals against strategic criteria.
  • Trade-off transparency — Make the cost of each choice visible.
  • Sequencing — Plan high-value work earlier, defer the rest.
  • Stakeholder power/interest mapping — Focus attention where influence is highest.
  • Formal sign-off — Obtain documented agreement on prioritization.
Example: Operations wants a manual repair tool (reduces their workload), while compliance wants automated sanctions screening (mandatory). The Senior Product Owner sequences the sanctions screening as the priority, then uses saved risk-credits to justify the repair tool in the next quarter.
Practice Task: For a payment project, map your stakeholders on a power/interest grid. Create a communication plan showing who receives what information, at what frequency, and through which channel. Draft a meeting agenda for a cross-functional prioritization session.
