AI-1 — Building Production AI Agents

The AI-India 1st Edition Study Companion

This book turns a one-day conference into a structured, self-paced learning path. It covers the ideas that were discussed on stage — agent frameworks, retrieval, tool calling, MCP, evaluation, inference engineering, guardrails, local AI, developer tooling, and careers — and expands them into explanations, comparisons, code-oriented labs, and review questions.

Originality note. Every session summary in this book has been rewritten from scratch in the author's own words from the event information supplied. No talk abstract, article, or biography text is reproduced verbatim. Framework and product names are used descriptively for educational purposes only. Individual speaker names and affiliations are deliberately omitted. This is an independent study guide and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the event organisers.

How to read this book

  • Parts I–II build the conceptual foundation: models, prompting, RAG, tools, and the major agent frameworks.
  • Part III explains how agents connect to the outside world through MCP and production integration patterns.
  • Part IV is the reliability and safety core: evaluation, self-improvement limits, guardrails, and observability.
  • Part V moves down the stack into inference, routing, local models, and on-device AI.
  • Part VI focuses on your daily workflow and career: AI coding assistants, token economics, and the hiring market.
  • Part VII is the event companion: the session schedule, the TypeScript workshop, and capstone projects.
