About DesiPay

DesiPay is a demo Android UPI payment app built by Hetansh Technologie. It uses the standard UPI deep-link flow (upi://pay) to hand control to your installed UPI app, so DesiPay itself never touches your money or bank credentials.

  • • Package: com.example.upipayments
  • • Requires: Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher
  • • A UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) must be installed to complete payments
  • • No special permissions or internet access required for transfers
Note: This is a demonstration build (version 1.0). Payments are processed entirely by the UPI app you choose. Always verify the recipient UPI ID before paying.
