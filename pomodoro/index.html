Pomodoro Timer

Android App

Stay productive with configurable focus/break intervals, alarms, and background timer.

Features

  • Configurable focus (default 25m) and break (default 5m) durations
  • Continuous cycling — auto-switches between focus and break sessions
  • Alarm ringtone + vibration on every session end
  • Runs in background with persistent notification
  • Pause, Resume, Reset, and Stop controls
